Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. About the Bot

NFAM Admin's Bot is a Discord bot that provides moderation, music playback, and security features for Discord servers.

2. Acceptable Use

Users must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines while using the bot. The bot must not be used for harassment, spam, or other harmful activities.

3. Moderation Features

The bot provides commands for warnings, timeouts, kicking, banning, and managing messages. Server administrators are responsible for configuring these features properly.

4. Honeypot Security

The Honeypot feature is designed to detect messages sent in a designated security channel. Depending on the server configuration, posting in this channel may result in an automatic timeout, kick, or ban.

5. Music Features

The bot provides music playback and queue management. Music availability depends on external services and technical conditions.

6. Service Availability

The bot is provided as available. Features may change, become temporarily unavailable, or be discontinued.

7. Appeals

Members who believe they were moderated incorrectly should contact their server administrators to request a review.

8. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated when the bot's features or policies change.

9. Contact

For questions about the bot, contact the NFAM development team through its designated support channel.
