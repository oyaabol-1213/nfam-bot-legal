NFAM Admin's Bot

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NFAM Admin's Bot processes information while providing moderation, music, and security features in Discord servers.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the bot may process:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs.
  • Channel IDs and bot configuration settings.
  • Moderation actions, including warnings, timeouts, kicks, and bans.
  • Honeypot detections and security logs.
  • Music commands, searches, and queue information.
  • Message content when needed to process commands or detect messages in the Honeypot channel.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to execute commands, provide music playback, manage moderation actions, detect activity in designated security channels, and maintain bot settings.

4. Data Storage

Some configuration settings and moderation records may be stored in the bot's local data files. Security logs may also be posted to designated Discord channels accessible to authorized server staff.

5. Data Retention

Stored settings and moderation records may remain until they are deleted by an authorized administrator or the bot operator. Discord messages and security logs are also subject to Discord's own retention and deletion processes.

6. Sharing Information

Information may be processed through Discord and services needed to provide music playback. Moderation and security logs may be visible to server administrators with access to the relevant channels.

7. Data Requests

Users may contact the bot operator to ask about stored information or request deletion of eligible records. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate moderation or security purposes.

8. Security

The bot operator aims to limit access to configuration files, authentication credentials, and moderation records. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the NFAM development team through its designated support channel.
